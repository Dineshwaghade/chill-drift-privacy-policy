Chill Drift — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Chill Drift ("the app", "the game") is developed by Waghade Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app interacts with, why, and the choices you have. Chill Drift is an offline endless-runner game — it does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or any other directly identifying information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally on your device only. This includes:

  • High scores and best distance
  • Collected in-game currency (snowflakes)
  • Unlocked and selected characters and boards
  • Sound, music, and haptics preferences

This data stays on your device. We do not operate a server that receives, stores, or has access to it, and it is removed if you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

Chill Drift shows interstitial ads between game sessions using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifier and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate, coarse location derived from it
  • Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and similar engagement signals)

This processing is governed by Google's own privacy policy and terms, not by us directly — we do not receive or store this data ourselves. You can learn more and review your options at:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use AdMob

On Android, you can view or reset your advertising ID, and opt out of personalized (interest-based) ads, from your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your Android version).

Children's privacy

Chill Drift is a general-audience casual game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it. Because the app serves ads via Google AdMob, ad personalization for any users identified as children is handled according to Google's own child-directed treatment and applicable regulations (such as COPPA).

Data security

Since gameplay data is stored only on your device and the app does not transmit it to us, there is no developer-operated server or database holding your game data to secure. Data handled by the advertising SDK is protected under Google's own security practices.
